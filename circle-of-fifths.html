The Circle of Fifths

The map of every key. Click a slice to see its key signature, scale, diatonic chords and closest neighbours — and hear it. Going clockwise adds a sharp; anticlockwise adds a flat.

Neighbouring keys share almost all their notes — that's why they sound good together. The inner ring shows each key's relative minor (same notes, different home).
