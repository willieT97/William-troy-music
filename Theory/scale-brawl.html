How this game helps

Hearing or seeing a short run of notes and playing it straight back is the start of learning tunes by ear and reading at sight. Scale Brawl turns it into a fight: the faster and cleaner you echo, the harder you hit.

The first bout stays on the white notes of C major, starting with just three notes. Each round adds notes and speed. Play on a MIDI keyboard, your laptop keys, or tap the keys on screen.

It works both ways. Block his notes and you earn shots to fire back, and now the Baron has to read and echo your phrase. Making up a short phrase on the spot is the first step to improvising.

Play a friend online: one of you makes a room, the other joins with its 4-letter code on their own device. Great for a lesson, a teacher against a pupil, or two pupils practising between lessons.

A band plays under the fight, so every note sits in a key and on a beat. In round 2 timing counts too: the Baron plays a bar of rhythm and you echo it in time, the way a teacher plays a phrase for you to copy.
