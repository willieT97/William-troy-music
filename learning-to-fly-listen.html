The Listening Lab 🎧

Play the pentatonic scale in any order along with the click — the lab listens and scores you on notes in the scale and rhythm. Single-note instruments.

🎧 Use wired headphones or earbuds. Bluetooth adds a delay (often ~⅕ second) that throws the rhythm scoring off — and headphones keep the click out of the mic.
