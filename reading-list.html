The books and papers behind the creativity workshops.

Seven shelves, read roughly in this order: first the books you can lift exercises straight out of, then the traditions that keep music in the body, the “way” traditions, somatics, the philosophy underneath it all, the research that backs it up, and finally the formats other people have used to teach creativity. Every entry has a link to read it — open-access papers and public-domain books load in full; the rest point to Open Library, which will lend you a copy where it can.
