Song Trainer

Chords · rhythm · theory

Learn a song by its chords, rhythm and theory. Hit play, strum along in time, then dig into the scales you can solo with.

Chords & theory only — no lyrics or melody. (Chord progressions aren't copyrightable.)

Songs load automatically from the songs/ folder. Drop a MuseScore export in there (File ▸ Export ▸ MusicXML, Uncompressed .musicxml) with chord symbols and a written rhythm — add a bass staff and a Drumset staff in the same file to play them along too. Repeats and 1st/2nd endings are followed. Or use ＋ MuseScore XML to try a one-off file.

How to hold each chord

A slash chord (written like C/E) has its lowest note played below the root — usually by the bass or another instrument. Switch to root position to play it as a plain chord.
