Reading · Voyage

Old Man and the C

A seafaring note-reading tale. Read each note to reel it in and sail from port to port, casting off in the Shallows and working out toward deeper water — a slow crossing rather than a quick round.

Walking Bass

Learning to Walk

Sit in with an old jazz cat and learn to walk a bassline from the ground up — roots and fifths, strong and weak beats, arpeggios and passing tones — from a country barn dance to the blues to a late-night cutting session on the changes.

Improvisation · Pentatonic

Learning to Fly

Learn to improvise from the very first note. Start with melodies you already know and the pentatonic scale, bend them into your own, trade calls and answers with your teacher, then solo over a groove — on your own instrument.

Take flight
