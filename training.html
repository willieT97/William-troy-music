Ear training · pitch

Pitch Practice

Pure note ear training — no game. Hear a short sequence off a reference tonic and name what you heard. Pick the length, choose pentatonic up to fully chromatic, and stay in C or move through every key.

Melody Trainer

Read a tune on the stave and play or sing it back — your mic listens, and the cursor only advances when you land the right pitch. Ukulele, guitar, banjo, piano or voice, and you can load any tune with ABC notation.
